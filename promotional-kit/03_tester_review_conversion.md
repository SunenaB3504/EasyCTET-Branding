# 🌟 Tester-to-Public Review Conversion Kit

This kit is for reaching out to your **12+ closed testing team**. Since they already tested the app, converting their feedback into **Public 5-Star Ratings & Reviews** is the single fastest way to give EasyCTET immediate social proof on Google Play.

---

## 📌 The Golden Step: How Testers Switch to Public Reviews

> **Important:** While enrolled in the closed test, their reviews remain "Private Developer Feedback". To make their review public, they simply tap **"Leave"** under the beta section in Google Play. **They do NOT need to uninstall the app!**

## 💬 Message Template 2: Direct WhatsApp to Testers (English)

```text
Hi everyone! 🌟

A huge thank you for actively testing EasyCTET over the past 14 days and helping us reach this milestone!

EasyCTET is now officially LIVE in production on Google Play Store! 🚀

Could you please take 30 seconds to drop us a 5-star rating and a public review?

Here is how to make your review public (no need to reinstall):
1. Open our Play Store link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.easyctet.app
2. Scroll to the "You're a beta tester" section and tap "Leave".
3. Refresh the page, and the public "Rate this app" (★★★★★) box will appear!
4. Tap 5 stars and leave a brief review about your experience.

Your review will help future teachers discover this ad-free study tool. Thank you so much for your support! 🙌
```
