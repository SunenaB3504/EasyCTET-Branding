## 💡 Quick Rules of Thumb (Do's & Don'ts)

✅ **DO:**
- Highlight that the first **150-question mock test is 100% FREE** with no phone number needed.
- Emphasize **100% Offline / Airplane Mode** capability (teachers and students love this!).
- Point out **Zero Ads** (no annoying popups).

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't spam the same group multiple times in a day.
- Don't push paid subscriptions. Simply explain that complete lifetime access is just a one-time ₹950 (no recurring monthly fees).
- Never send bulk unsolicited spam messages that could risk your WhatsApp account being flagged.
