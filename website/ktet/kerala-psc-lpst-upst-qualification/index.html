Kerala PSC LPST & UPST Qualification Guide: KTET vs CTET Rules

Published: October 2026 · Source: Kerala PSC Notification No. 239–241/2025 & G.O.(P) No. 6/2024/G.Edn · Verified by EasyKTET Research Team

The Definitive Rule: Under Kerala Government Order G.O.(P) No. 6/2024/G.Edn (dated 01.02.2024), CTET Paper 1 is legally recognized as equivalent to KTET Category 1 for LPST, and CTET Paper 2 is recognized as equivalent to KTET Category 2 for UPST appointments. You do not need to retake KTET if you already hold a valid qualifying CTET certificate.

Understanding Government Order G.O.(P) No. 6/2024/G.Edn

For several years, Kerala teaching candidates faced uncertainty regarding whether the Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) conducted by CBSE would be accepted for state government and aided school vacancies announced by the Kerala Public Service Commission (KPSC).

On February 1, 2024, the General Education (J) Department of the Government of Kerala issued G.O.(P) No. 6/2024/G.Edn. This landmark order formally amended the Kerala Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Rules, 2011:

Key Provisions of G.O.(P) No. 6/2024:

  • Candidates possessing a valid CTET Paper 1 qualification are granted equivalency with KTET Category 1 for appointments to Lower Primary School Teacher (LPST / LPSA) posts.
  • Candidates possessing a valid CTET Paper 2 qualification are granted equivalency with KTET Category 2 for appointments to Upper Primary School Teacher (UPST / UPSA) posts.
  • This equivalency was formally adopted by the Kerala Public Service Commission and incorporated directly into Gazette notifications (including Category No. 239–241/2025).

B.Ed vs D.El.Ed in Kerala PSC: What Every Candidate Must Know

Following the Supreme Court of India's ruling in Devesh Sharma v. Union of India (Civil Appeal No. 5068/2023), state-level qualification rules underwent a significant shift:

  • For LPST (Classes 1–5): B.Ed graduates are completely disqualified from primary teaching appointments in Kerala. Only candidates possessing a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / TTC) meet the threshold. If you hold a B.Ed and cleared CTET Paper 1, you cannot use it for Kerala LPST. For full legal background, read our Supreme Court B.Ed Eligibility Breakdown.
  • For UPST (Classes 6–8): Both D.El.Ed and B.Ed graduates remain fully eligible, provided they hold a Bachelor's degree and have passed either KTET Category 2 or CTET Paper 2.

Pass Mark Standards for Kerala PSC Verification

When presenting your qualifying certificate during Kerala PSC One Time Verification (OTR):

1. If Presenting KTET:

The qualifying mark established by the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan applies directly:

  • General Category: 60% (90 out of 150 marks).
  • OBC / OEC / SEBC / SC / ST: 55% (82 out of 150 marks).
  • Differently Abled (PwD): 50% (75 out of 150 marks).

For complete category rules and rounding details, see our comprehensive guide: KTET Category 1, 2, 3 Pass Mark Ethra.

2. If Presenting CTET:

CBSE awards a uniform CTET passing mark of 60% (90/150). However, where state recruitment rules permit a 5% relaxation for reserved categories, Kerala PSC accepts qualifying marksheets issued to reserved candidates meeting the state reservation benchmark. For all-India comparison, see CTET Passing Marks by Employer & State.

Syllabus Overlap: CTET vs KTET

Although CTET is accepted for LPST and UPST, the exam format and focus differ substantially:

  • Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP): Very high overlap (~85%). Preparing Piaget, Vygotsky, and Kohlberg will serve you equally well in both tests.
  • Languages: KTET mandates Malayalam (or designated mother tongue) as Language I, focusing heavily on Kerala school curriculum pedagogy and Malayalam literature/grammar. CTET offers 20 language choices with pure unseen passage comprehension.
  • EVS & Social Studies: KTET integrates Kerala state SCERT textbook themes (local history, geography, environmental issues) not found in central NCERT-focused CTET papers.

Does CTET certificate validity expire for Kerala PSC recruitment?

No. Under NCTE notification and Government of Kerala orders, TET certificates (both CTET and KTET) possess lifetime validity. Qualified candidates do not need to re-appear simply to renew validity.
