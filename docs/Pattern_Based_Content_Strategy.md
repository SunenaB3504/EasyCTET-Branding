## 1. Strategic Foundation: The "Anti-Clickbait" Authority

Most Indian EdTech content relies on manufactured panic (e.g., *"BIG BREAKING NEWS: CTET 2026 CANCELLED??"*) to capture cheap clicks and harvest phone numbers.

Our content strategy operates on the opposite principle: **Radical Truth & Instant Utility**.
* We answer the exact question in the first 2 sentences.
* We display the official government data in clean, responsive tables.
* We require zero sign-up, zero phone numbers, and show zero popup ads.
* **The Conversion Hook:** Once the candidate experiences our calm, accurate answer, we offer the natural next step: *"Tired of conflicting answers and spam calls? Practice 15+ years of verified question papers 100% offline in EasyCTET."*

### Pillar 1: The "Borderline Passing Mark" Clarity Engine (506 Keywords)
* **Candidate Mindset:** Extreme anxiety about scoring 82 vs 90, reservation categories (OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and state vs. central relaxation rules.
* **Core Target Queries:**
  * `ctet passing marks for obc 2026`
  * `ktet cat 2 pass mark ethra`
  * `uptet me 82 number pass hai ya fail`
  * `bihar bpsc tre 4.0 me ctet marks kitne chahiye`
  * `is 82 pass in ctet for obc female candidate`

#### Content Execution Playbook:
1. **On `EasyCTET.com` (Static Web Page):**
   * **Headline:** *"CTET Passing Marks for OBC / SC / ST / General (2026 Official Rules)"*
   * **Opening:** The direct factual answer: *"CBSE sets a uniform qualifying benchmark of 60% (90 out of 150 marks). However, under NCTE guidelines, appointing authorities (KVS, DSSSB, State Governments) apply a 55% relaxation (82.5 marks, accepted as 82) for reserved categories according to their own recruitment rules."*
   * **Visual Asset:** A color-coded **State & Employer Concession Matrix** (showing CBSE Benchmark, KVS/NVS norms, Delhi DSSSB outsider rules, and Bihar BPSC TRE gender concessions).
   * **The Nuance Explainer:** Clarifying the cross-state rule (e.g., *"Why an 82-mark OBC candidate is valid for Central KVS, but treated as General in Delhi DSSSB if holding an outside-state certificate"*).
2. **On `@easyctetofficial` (YouTube Shorts):**
   * **Hook (0–3s):** *"Scored 82 marks in CTET? Whether you qualify depends on the state and recruiting board. Here is how it actually works."*
   * **Body (3–12s):** Quick breakdown of the 60% CBSE baseline vs 55% employer concession with clean visual text.
   * **CTA (12–15s):** *"Check your offline test score instantly in the EasyCTET app. Download on Google Play."*

#### Content Execution Playbook:
1. **On `EasyCTET.com` (Static Web Page):**

## 4. Execution Discipline

By anchoring all future content to these 5 empirical pillars, we ensure that:
1. **Zero time is wasted:** We never produce content on topics candidates aren't searching for.
2. **Every piece has a purpose:** Web captures search intent, PDFs share organically in teacher groups, and Shorts capture video search.
3. **All roads lead to the app:** Every single piece of content logically and respectfully guides the teacher to download **EasyCTET** or **EasyKTET**.
