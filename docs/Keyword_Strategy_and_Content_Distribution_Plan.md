# Operational Blueprint: 6-Step Ground-Truth Keyword & Content Distribution Pipeline
### Easy Education Technologies (EasyCTET / EasyKTET Ecosystem)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This document outlines the end-to-end, zero-cost content manufacturing and distribution supply chain for **EasyCTET** and **EasyKTET**. 

Rather than relying on paid advertisements, high-profile campaigns, or speculative keyword guessing, our digital marketing operates as an **asymmetric, data-driven content pipeline**. We first listen to the real, unfiltered questions teachers and aspirants search online (especially in code-mixed vernacular languages like Manglish and Hinglish), expand those questions algorithmically, group them into content clusters, map each query to its highest-converting media format, and distribute the assets silently across organic web, video, and social channels.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       THE 6-STEP CONTENT SUPPLY CHAIN                         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  [ Step 1: Discover ] ──▶ Listen to real users (YouTube comments, PAA, Forums)
           │
  [ Step 2: Expand ]   ──▶ Seed Algorithm (Code-mixing, Manglish/Hinglish markers)
           │
  [ Step 3: Cluster ]  ──▶ Group into 4 Core Intent Clusters
           │
  [ Step 4: Map ]      ──▶ Match Intent to Format (Web vs Short vs PDF vs Hub)
           │
  [ Step 5: Produce ]  ──▶ Automated Manufacturing (Pure HTML, Remotion, TTS)
           │
  [ Step 6: Distribute]──▶ Stealth Distribution (EasyCTET.com, YouTube, WhatsApp)
```

## 2. Step 1: Ground-Truth Keyword Discovery (The Listening Phase)

Before generating any seed list, we harvest unfiltered human language directly from the digital gathering places of Indian teacher aspirants:

1. **Top Educator YouTube Comment Sections:**
   * **Target Channels:** *Sachin Academy*, *Let's LEARN (Himanshi Singh)*, *Entri App (Kerala)*, *Teaching Wallah*.
   * **Target Discussions:** Top comments under recent live streams and exam-analysis videos where students ask real, anxious edge-case questions.
   * **Harvesting Focus:** Raw phrasing, typos, emotional anxiety, and regional language mixing.
2. **Google "People Also Ask" (PAA) Engines:**
   * Query bare root entities (`CTET`, `KTET`) on Google.
   * Extract the dynamic question hierarchy directly from Google's semantic index.
3. **Quora India & Public Telegram Study Groups:**
   * Search `?` and `pass mark` in active 20,000+ member TET study groups.
   * Identify recurring debates and unresolved candidate dilemmas.

**Milestone Output:** A raw discovery corpus of ~200 real, unfiltered candidate questions reflecting actual colloquial vocabulary.

## 3. Step 2: Algorithmic Seeding & Code-Mixing Expansion

We translate the real-world question structures discovered in Step 1 into a mathematical generator that expands across all exams and subjects.

### The Code-Mixing / Transliteration Engine:
Corporate competitors target only textbook English. We gain uncontested organic rankings by targeting **Romanized Vernacular (Transliteration)**:

* **Kerala / Manglish Root Markers:**
  * `{exam} cat {N} pass mark ethra`
  * `{exam} pass aavan ethra mark venam`
  * `is b.ed eligible for {exam} aano`
  * `{exam} examil negative mark undo`
  * `d el ed kazhinjavarkku {exam} ezhuthamo`
  * `{exam} previous year question paper download malayalam`
* **Hindi Belt / Hinglish Root Markers:**
  * `{exam} pass hone ke liye kitne number chahiye {category} ko`
  * `{exam} me 82 number pass hai ya fail`
  * `kya b.ed wale {exam} paper 1 de sakte hai`
  * `{exam} ki taiyari bina coaching ke kaise kare`
  * `{exam} certificate ki validity kitne saal hoti hai`

### Mathematical Multiplier Matrix:
$$\text{Exam Entity} \times \text{Core Subject Pillar} \times \text{Code-Mixed Question Marker} \times \text{Alphabet Expansion (a–z)}$$

**Milestone Output:** A clean, deduplicated database of 1,000+ verified long-tail search queries with URL slugs.

## 4. Step 3: Semantic Clustering

To prevent chaotic, disconnected content creation, queries are organized into **4 Master Content Clusters**:

```
                       [ Master Keyword Database ]
                                    │
    ┌─────────────────┬─────────────┴─────────────┬─────────────────┐
    ▼                 ▼                           ▼                 ▼
[ Cluster A ]   [ Cluster B ]               [ Cluster C ]     [ Cluster D ]
Cutoffs &       Legal Eligibility &         Child Development Question Papers,
Passing Marks   Qualifications              & Pedagogy (CDP)  Syllabus & Overlap
```

* **Cluster A: Passing Marks & Cutoff Rules:**
  * Specific mark thresholds (82 vs 90 marks out of 150).
  * Category relaxations (OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD).
  * State vs. Central recruitment validity.
* **Cluster B: Legal Eligibility & Qualification:**
  * B.Ed vs. D.El.Ed eligibility post-Supreme Court ruling.
  * Appearing vs. qualified semester rules.
  * Degree percentage minimums.
* **Cluster C: Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP):**
  * Core theorists: Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Thorndike, Skinner, Pavlov.
  * Inclusive education, CCE, and learning disabilities.
* **Cluster D: Question Papers, Syllabus & Overlap:**
  * Previous year question papers with official answer keys.
  * Curricular overlap between CTET and State TETs.

## 6. Step 5: Automated Content Manufacturing

Content is produced without expensive manual labor or agency retainers by utilizing lean software automation:

1. **Static Web Page Engine:**
   * Lightweight, zero-framework HTML/CSS.
   * Under 35 KB per page for instant mobile loading on rural 3G/4G connections.
   * Embedded JSON-LD `FAQPage` schema to win Google Rich Snippets.
   * Native dark mode support and responsive design.
2. **Automated Remotion Video Pipeline:**
   * Automated rendering of 9:16 vertical video shorts from structured question JSONs.
   * High-retention visual mechanics: animated 10-second countdown timer, karaoke-style subtitles.
   * Voiceover generated via **Azure Neural TTS** (Hindi/Malayalam) and **Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD**.
3. **PDF Generator:**
   * Scripted generation of clean, printable 2-page study sheets with embedded QR codes.

## 8. Master Keyword Schema (Data Architecture)

All discovered keywords will be maintained in a structured CSV (`all_india_tet_master_keywords.csv`) utilizing the following relational schema:

```csv
keyword_id,raw_query,target_exam,cluster_id,content_format,clean_slug,target_url,video_hook,pdf_title,app_cta_copy
```

* **`keyword_id`**: Standardized identifier (e.g., `KTET_CUTOFF_OBC_001`).
* **`raw_query`**: Exact user query (e.g., `ktet cat 2 pass mark ethra`).
* **`target_exam`**: Target exam entity (e.g., `KTET`).
* **`cluster_id`**: One of `CLUSTER_A_CUTOFF`, `CLUSTER_B_ELIGIBILITY`, `CLUSTER_C_PEDAGOGY`, `CLUSTER_D_PYQ`.
* **`content_format`**: `WEB_PAGE`, `VIDEO_SHORT`, `PDF_CHEAT_SHEET`, or `PILLAR_HUB`.
* **`clean_slug`**: URL-safe, regex-sanitized path (e.g., `ktet-cat-2-pass-mark-ethra`).
* **`target_url`**: Canonical URL on `EasyCTET.com`.
* **`video_hook`**: First 3-second hook for Remotion audio/video script.
* **`pdf_title`**: Title for WhatsApp/Telegram PDF distribution.
* **`app_cta_copy`**: Contextual call-to-action promoting the offline app.
