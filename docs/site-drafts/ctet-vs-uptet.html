CTET vs UPTET Syllabus Comparison: What Overlaps & Super TET Rules

Published: October 2026 · Source: UPESSC UPTET Primary/Upper Primary Syllabi & Advt. 05/2026 · Verified by EasyCTET Research Team

The Core Verdict: About 80% of the CTET Paper 1 syllabus directly overlaps with UPTET Paper 1 (63 out of 79 topics match or are closely similar). For Paper 2, the overlap is 76% (94 of 124 topics across streams). If you prepare for CTET, your core pedagogy, mathematics, and EVS foundational concepts are thoroughly covered. What you must add for UPTET is heavy, explicit Hindi and English grammar, classic learning theorists (Thorndike, Pavlov, Skinner), and UP state administration.

संक्षेप में: अगर आप CTET की तैयारी कर रहे हैं, तो UPTET का 80% पाठ्यक्रम पहले से कवर हो जाता है। आपको केवल व्याकरण (संधि, समास, रस, छंद, अलंकार), अधिगम के शास्त्रीय सिद्धांत, और उत्तर प्रदेश सामान्य ज्ञान को अतिरिक्त रूप से पढ़ना होगा।

Can You Apply for UP Super TET with CTET?

Yes, absolutely. Many Uttar Pradesh teaching aspirants worry whether they must clear UPTET specifically before sitting for the state's Assistant Teacher recruitment (commonly referred to as UP Super TET).

Official Statutory Position (UPESSC Advt. No. 05/2026, Clause 7):

The recruitment rules for Assistant Teacher (Primary) in Uttar Pradesh specify that the candidate must have qualified in a Teacher Eligibility Test conducted by either the Government of Uttar Pradesh (UPTET) or the Government of India (CTET).

If you have already scored qualifying marks in CTET Paper 1 (90 for General, 82 for UP OBC/SC/ST), you are 100% eligible to apply for UP Super TET without waiting for the next UPTET cycle.

Subject-by-Subject Overlap Analysis

1. Child Development & Pedagogy (30 Marks) — 75% Overlap

The core conceptual framework of child growth, heredity, environment, and inclusive education is identical. However, UPTET places greater emphasis on rote theoretical definitions and named psychologists:

  • Common to both: Piaget, Kohlberg, Vygotsky, inclusive education for children with special needs (CWSN), learning factors, and motivation.
  • What UPTET adds: Detailed questions on Thorndike's Connectionism (Trial and Error theory & laws of learning), Pavlov's Classical Conditioning, Skinner's Operant Conditioning, and Kohler's Insight Learning. You must memorize specific experimental animals, reinforcement schedules, and learning curve types.

2. Language I: Hindi (30 Marks) — High Contrast

This is where CTET and UPTET diverge the most:

  • In CTET: 15 marks are based on unseen comprehension prose/poetry, and 15 marks test language teaching methodology (LSRW skills, multilingualism, error analysis). Explicit grammar is rarely tested beyond basic contextual word meanings.
  • In UPTET: Hindi is heavily grammar-driven. You must prepare Varnamala (swar, vyanjan, uccharan sthan), Sandhi (Swar, Vyanjan, Visarga), Samas, Alankar, Ras, Chhand, Tatsam/Tadbhav, idioms/proverbs (Muhavare/Lokoktiyan), and famous Hindi poets and their canonical works (Kavi evam unki rachnayein).

3. Language II: English / Sanskrit / Urdu (30 Marks)

While CTET tests Language II through reading comprehension and 15 marks of pedagogy, the UPTET Language II syllabus lists content only:

  • Parts of speech (Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction).
  • Active and Passive voice transformation.
  • Direct and Indirect speech (Narration).
  • Tenses, Articles, Punctuation, and Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, One-word substitution).

4. Mathematics (30 Marks in Paper 1; 60 in Paper 2 Stream) — 95% Overlap

CTET mathematics preparation covers UPTET almost entirely. Number systems, basic operations, fractions, geometry, measurement, and data handling are common. In Paper 2, UPTET includes commercial mathematics (banking, simple & compound interest, taxation) and basic probability, which require a quick formula review.

5. Environmental Studies (30 Marks in Paper 1) — 95% Overlap

The six NCERT themes (Family & Friends, Food, Shelter, Water, Travel, Things We Make and Do) form the bulk of both exams. UPTET adds questions on Indian geography, Uttar Pradesh state geography, local fairs (melas), Panchayati Raj, and environmental conservation acts.

B.Ed vs D.El.Ed Eligibility in Uttar Pradesh

Following the Supreme Court of India's judgment in Devesh Sharma v. Union of India (Civil Appeal No. 5068/2023):

  • Primary Level (Classes 1–5): B.Ed graduates are completely disqualified from primary teacher recruitment in UP. Only 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / BTC) holders can apply for Primary UPTET and Primary Super TET.
  • Upper Primary Level (Classes 6–8): B.Ed degree holders remain eligible for UPTET Paper 2, Junior High School teacher posts, and UP TGT recruitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

क्या CTET पास करने के बाद UPTET देना जरूरी है?

नहीं। उत्तर प्रदेश शिक्षा सेवा चयन आयोग (UPESSC) के सहायक अध्यापक (प्राइमरी सुपर टीईटी) भर्ती नियमों (Advt. No. 05/2026, Clause 7) के तहत केंद्र सरकार द्वारा आयोजित CTET और राज्य सरकार द्वारा आयोजित UPTET दोनों समान रूप से मान्य हैं। यदि आपने CTET Paper 1 पास कर लिया है, तो आप सीधे सुपर टीईटी के लिए पात्र हैं।

CTET और UPTET के सिलेबस में मुख्य अंतर क्या है?

मुख्य अंतर व्याकरण (Grammar) और बाल विकास (CDP) के सिद्धांतों में है। CTET में पेडागॉजी (शिक्षण शास्त्र) और अपठित गद्यांश प्रमुख होते हैं, जबकि UPTET में शुद्ध हिंदी व्याकरण (संधि, समास, अलंकार, रस, छंद, कवियों की रचनाएं) और अंग्रेजी व्याकरण के सीधे प्रश्न पूछे जाते हैं। साथ ही UPTET में थार्नडाइक, पावलव और स्किनर जैसे अधिगम सिद्धांतकारों पर विशेष जोर रहता है।

UPTET में पासिंग मार्क्स कितने होते हैं?

UPTET में कुल 150 अंकों में से सामान्य (General) वर्ग के लिए 60% (90 अंक) और OBC, SC, ST, PwD वर्ग के लिए 55% (82 अंक) क्वालिफाइंग मार्क निर्धारित हैं। विस्तृत विवरण के लिए देखें: CTET और TET पासिंग मार्क्स गाइड।

क्या B.Ed वाले UP Primary Teacher (सुपर टीईटी) के लिए पात्र हैं?

नहीं। सुप्रीम कोर्ट के देवेश शर्मा बनाम भारत संघ फैसले के बाद उत्तर प्रदेश में प्राथमिक विद्यालयों (कक्षा 1 से 5) में शिक्षक बनने के लिए केवल D.El.Ed / BTC धारक ही पात्र हैं। B.Ed डिग्री धारक केवल उच्च प्राथमिक (UPTET Paper 2 / Junior) और टीजीटी पदों के लिए पात्र हैं।

Does CTET certificate have lifetime validity for UP recruitment?

Yes. Per National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) notification and Government of Uttar Pradesh orders, both CTET and UPTET certificates carry lifetime validity retrospective from 2011.
