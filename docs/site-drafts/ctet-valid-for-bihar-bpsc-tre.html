Is CTET Valid for Bihar BPSC TRE 4.0? Official Passing Marks & Rules

Yes, CTET is fully valid for Bihar BPSC TRE. Candidates who qualify CTET Paper 1 (D.El.Ed only) or Paper 2 do not require BTET. Bihar government provides special mark relaxations for women and reserved categories.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can other state female candidates apply in Bihar with 82 marks in CTET?

No. The 82-mark relaxation for females applies only to Bihar domicile residents. Other state candidates (both male and female) require 90 marks (60%).

Is B.Ed eligible for BPSC TRE Class 1 to 5?

No. In accordance with Supreme Court directives, Bihar Education Department allows only D.El.Ed candidates for Primary (Class 1-5).

Does Bihar BPSC conduct its own TET?

Bihar accepts CTET for primary and middle schools (Classes 1–8). For High School (Classes 9–10) and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12), candidates must qualify Bihar STET.
