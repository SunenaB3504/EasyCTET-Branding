Is B.Ed Eligible for CTET Paper 1? Supreme Court Ruling Explained

No. Following the Supreme Court of India's landmark judgment (August 11, 2023), B.Ed degree holders are not eligible for Primary Teacher (Classes 1–5) Paper 1. B.Ed candidates can only apply for CTET Paper 2 (Classes 6–8) and secondary teaching recruitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can B.Ed candidates appear for CTET Paper 1 just for practice?

CBSE does not issue Paper 1 eligibility certificates to B.Ed candidates. Even if attempted, the certificate is legally invalid for primary school teacher recruitment.

Can D.El.Ed candidates apply for CTET Paper 2?

Yes! D.El.Ed candidates who also hold a Bachelor's Degree (BA, B.Sc, B.Com) are fully eligible to apply for CTET Paper 2.

Are 1st year B.Ed students eligible to appear for CTET Paper 2?

Yes. As per NCTE notification, any candidate who is actively pursuing an approved teacher education program (even from day 1) is eligible to appear.
